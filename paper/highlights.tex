\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Imputation, feature selection and stacking were all fitted inside validation.
  \item Stacking matched but did not beat logistic regression (CV AUC 0.843 vs 0.842).
  \item RFECV cut inputs by 25\% and kept glucose, BMI and family-history terms.
  \item Class weighting overestimated risk; calibration must be reported.
  \item Sensitivity-targeted thresholds transferred from development to hold-out data.
\end{itemize}
